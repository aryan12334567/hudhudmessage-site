The basics

How a WhatsApp bill is built

Meta sets a price for every message your brand sends. Your provider (AiSensy, Interakt, WATI, Gupshup or another) delivers it and adds its own charge on top. This is Meta's India price list from 1 October 2026.

  • Meta charges when a message is delivered, not when it's sent.
  • Messages your customers send you are free.
  • Customers who arrive from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad can open a 72-hour free window.

Where the extra hides

Three places money leaks out of the bill

None of these show up as a line on your invoice. That's why most brands never see them.

  1. 01

    The extra on every message

    Providers add their charge on top of Meta's price. Published price lists we checked in October 2026 go from Meta's own price to about 26% above it on marketing, and up to about 74% above on utility. Some extra is fair, because your provider runs the software. The question is whether yours is fair for a brand your size.

  2. 02

    Messages billed at the wrong price

    A shipping update is utility, ₹0.115. Add one discount line and Meta can class it as marketing, ₹0.8631. Same message, 7.5 times the price, every single time it goes out.

    ₹0.115₹0.8631
  3. 03

    Price changes nobody noticed

    Meta changed WhatsApp prices in July 2025, January 2026 and October 2026. Providers change their plans too. Most brands find out months later, from the bank statement.

What we measure

Sending fewer messages is easy. Helping more customers for less is the real job.

We never cut a message that brings an order or answers a customer. We track two numbers every month: what it costs to resolve one customer, and what it costs to win one order. If those don't improve, we haven't done our job.

FAQ

Questions founders ask us

Are you a WhatsApp provider like AiSensy or WATI?

No. We never send messages. You keep your current provider and setup. We only look at what you're charged and help bring it down.

Do I have to switch providers?

No. Most of the time the fix is a better price from your current provider, or templates moved to the right category.

Isn't some extra on top of Meta's price normal?

Yes. Your provider charges for its software and support. The question is whether your price is fair for a brand your size. That's what the comparison shows you.

Can't I just put my bill into ChatGPT?

You can, and it will tell you part of the story. It can't tell you what brands like yours actually pay, and it won't negotiate with your provider or get templates re-approved by Meta. That's the part we do.

Will cutting costs hurt my sales?

We don't cut messages that bring orders or answer customers. We track your cost per order and cost per resolved customer every month, so you can see sales held while cost fell.

What happens to the invoice I share?

We use it only to read your bill and, without your brand's name, to build the comparison. Comparisons show only groups of 8 or more similar brands. We never need your customers' personal data.

How do you measure the saving you charge for?

Before we start, we record your price per message for each message type from your last 3 invoices. Each month we compare against that. You sign off the numbers before we bill.

What changed on 1 October 2026?

Order updates sent while a customer chat is open now cost ₹0.115 each. The first 1,000 support replies per number each month stay free, and messages from customers are free. Marketing messages are still the biggest part of most bills.
